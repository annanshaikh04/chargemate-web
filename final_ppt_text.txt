--- ppt/slides/slide3.xml ---
Product Concept  (adapter + power bank combo) Concept Overview An all-in-one adapter + power bank system designed to eliminate the friction of managing cables, plug types, and battery life while on the go. Core Idea “One device. Every plug. Always powered.”  A compact, modular charging solution that integrates: Cable conversion  Power conversion  Portable battery supply 

--- ppt/slides/slide4.xml ---
Basic  Components Universal Compatibility  Supports global plug types (US, EU, UK, etc.)  Multi-Device Charging Up to 8 ports for simultaneous use  5 interchangeable female ports stored within the device Interchangeable Cable Ends USB-C base with attachable ends (USB-A, Lightning, etc.)  Built-In Power Bank Detachable battery for charging without outlets  Retractable & Organized Design Cables and plugs stored within the device (no loose parts)

Real-world Value Eliminating Everyday Friction Removes the need to carry multiple chargers, adapters, and cables  Time & Convenience One device replaces 5+ essential charging items  Faster setup, no searching, untangling, or switching adapters  Reliability & Preparedness Ensures users stay powered in critical moments (travel, meetings, emergencies)  Cost Efficiency Over Time Replaces the need to purchase multiple adapters and chargers  Reduces loss/replacement costs from forgotten or misplaced accessories
